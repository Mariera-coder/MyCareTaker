# About INKAHOME

INKAHOME is a role-based property management system designed to simplify estate operations for small teams and growing property portfolios.

## Purpose

The application helps administrators, owners, caretakers, and tenants work from a shared system instead of disconnected tools. It is designed to support everyday operational tasks while keeping permissions and data access aligned to each role.

## Roles supported

- `ADMIN` manages platform setup, approvals, users, houses, and assignments
- `OWNER` oversees estates, reports, and complaint approvals
- `CARETAKER` manages assigned estates, reviews complaints, and confirms payments
- `TENANT` accesses tenancy context, submits payments, and raises complaints

## Key workflows

- registration requests and admin approval
- estate and house setup
- tenancy assignment and vacancy updates
- complaint lifecycle management
- payment collection visibility and reporting
- authenticated session handling with secure cookie-based auth

## Positioning

This repository is a strong foundation for an internal property operations platform, pilot deployment, or portfolio management MVP. It is structured to be extendable as the business rules grow.
